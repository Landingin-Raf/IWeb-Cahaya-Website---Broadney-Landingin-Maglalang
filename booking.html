Good to Know

Booking policies

Deposit

A 50% deposit reserves your date. The remaining balance is payable on arrival, in cash or by bank transfer.

Rescheduling

One free reschedule is allowed if requested at least 7 days before your booked date, subject to availability.

Cancellation

Deposits are non-refundable but may be applied to a rescheduled date within 60 days.

House rules

No smoking indoors, no pets, and quiet hours after 10:00 PM out of respect for neighbours.

Capacity

Rates cover up to 15 guests. Extra guests are ₱300 each, with a strict maximum of 20.

Rainy Day Promo

Book two nights or more and enjoy 5% off your total rate.

Questions

Before you book

How do I know my date is confirmed?

Your date is confirmed only once we've replied to your request and received the deposit. Until then the date stays open to other guests.

Is the whole property really private?

It is. There are no shared spaces and no other guests booked alongside you — the pool, garden and villa are exclusively yours.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Day tours run from 2:00 PM to 10:00 PM. Overnight stays check in at 3:00 PM and check out at 12:00 NN the next day.

How many guests can we bring?

All rates include up to 15 guests. You can bring up to 5 more at ₱300 per person, for a maximum of 20.

Where exactly is Cahaya?

Lot 9 Block 46 Kagandahan Street, Duquit, Mabalacat, Pampanga — minutes from Clark and major expressway exits.
