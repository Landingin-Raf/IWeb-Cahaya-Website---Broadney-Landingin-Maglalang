Welcome to Cahaya

A quiet corner of Bali, minutes from home.

Cahaya means light — and that is what the whole place was built around. Open-air living, warm timber, thatched roofing and greenery in every direction, tucked away in Mabalacat, Pampanga.

A slow weekend for two, a family reunion, or an intimate celebration — the property is yours alone for the duration of your stay. No shared spaces, no crowds, just the pool and the sound of the leaves.

  • Exclusive use of the entire property
  • Up to 15 guests included, maximum of 20
  • Minutes from Clark and major expressway exits
